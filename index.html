Good to know

Questions before you choose.

Can I use My Helm outside New Zealand?

My Helm's GPS, Anchor Watch and Saved Places tools may operate in other locations, but its detailed LINZ chart coverage and chart catalogue are for New Zealand waters. My Helm is currently sold and supported as a New Zealand edition. Other regional editions may be developed in the future.

Does everything work offline?

My Vessel Log works fully offline once loaded. My Helm itself, its saved information, GPS tools and Anchor Watch can remain available once loaded offline, but LINZ chart images need an internet connection. Previously viewed images may appear from the browser cache, but this is not guaranteed.

Where is my information stored?

Your maintenance records and saved places are stored in the browser on your own device. There are no customer accounts and no cloud syncing. Both apps include backup and restore tools so you can keep a safe copy or move your data.

Is My Helm a replacement for a chartplotter?

No. My Helm is a supplementary reference tool. It is not a substitute for current official charts, approved navigation equipment, proper watchkeeping or a dedicated anchor alarm. Always navigate using appropriate equipment and current information.

Can I use the apps on more than one device?

Yes. Open your app link on a phone, tablet or computer. Because data stays on each device, use Backup and Restore when you want to transfer your records or saved places between devices.
