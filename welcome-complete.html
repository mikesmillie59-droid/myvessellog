Quick setup guide

My Vessel Log

  1. Enter your boat and engine details.
  2. Check the starter maintenance schedule.

My Helm

  1. Open Settings and select LINZ charts.
  2. Enter your LINZ Data Service key and load the catalogue.
  3. Test GPS, voice and Anchor Watch.
  4. Add Helm to your home screen.
My Helm chart coverage: the detailed LINZ chart catalogue is for New Zealand waters and chart images require internet access. GPS tools, Anchor Watch and saved places remain available after Helm has loaded offline.
My Helm is a supplementary reference aid and is not a substitute for current official charts, approved navigation equipment, proper watchkeeping or a dedicated anchor alarm.
