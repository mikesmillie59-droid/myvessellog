Important safety information

A companion—not primary navigation.

My Helm is a supplementary reference aid. It is not a substitute for current official charts, a compliant chartplotter, prudent watchkeeping or a dedicated anchor alarm.

Can I use My Helm outside New Zealand?

Its GPS, Anchor Watch and Saved Places tools may operate elsewhere, but detailed LINZ chart coverage and the chart catalogue are for New Zealand waters. My Helm is currently sold and supported only as a New Zealand Edition.

Do filters disable alerts?

No. Saved Places filters change which categories are shown on the map and in the list. Enabled proximity alerts remain active for places hidden by a filter.

Is Anchor Watch affected by Saved Places filters?

No. Anchor Watch is an independent safety tool and remains visible and active separately from Saved Places categories.

Does it replace a dedicated anchor alarm?

No. Treat Anchor Watch as a supplementary aid and maintain prudent watchkeeping and appropriate dedicated equipment.
