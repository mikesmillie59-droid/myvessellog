Common questions

Before you choose.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. My Vessel Log works fully offline once it has loaded on the device.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Open the app link on each phone, tablet or computer. Data is stored separately on each device, so use Backup and Restore when moving records between them.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. My Vessel Log is a one-off US$29 purchase with no subscription.

Can I change the starter maintenance schedule?

Yes. The starter schedule is only a convenient starting point. You can add, edit or remove tasks and set your own service intervals.
