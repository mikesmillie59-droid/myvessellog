First-time chart setup

Helm uses LINZ reference charts for New Zealand waters.
1
Open Helm and tap Settings.
2
Choose LINZ reference charts as the map background.
3
Enter your LINZ Data Service access key when requested, then load or refresh the New Zealand chart catalogue.
4
Return to Navigation, choose your area and test GPS and voice before relying on the app underway.

Add it to your home screen

This gives Helm its own icon and allows it to open full-screen.
1
Open Helm in Safari and tap the Share button.
2
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3
Tap Add. The Helm icon will appear on your home screen.
1
Open Helm in Chrome and tap the three-dot menu.
2
Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
3
Confirm by tapping Add or Install.
Internet and offline use: LINZ chart images require an internet connection. GPS tools, Anchor Watch and saved places remain available after Helm has loaded offline, but cached chart images are not guaranteed.
