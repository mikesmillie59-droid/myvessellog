Payment and access

Good to know.

Are these subscriptions?

No. Each price is a one-off payment with no monthly or annual fee.

How do I receive the app?

After payment, Stripe sends you to the matching welcome page where you can open the purchased app and follow the home-screen setup instructions.

Can I purchase the bundle outside New Zealand?

My Vessel Log is available worldwide, but the current bundle includes My Helm New Zealand Edition. The bundle is therefore intended for boat owners using New Zealand waters.

Where is my app data stored?

Your records and Saved Places are stored locally in your device's browser. Use each app's Backup and Restore tools to keep a safe copy or move data.
